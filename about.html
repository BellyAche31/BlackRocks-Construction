Our Story

Restoration built on craftsmanship, not shortcuts

BlackRocks Construction was built on a simple belief: natural stone deserves to be treated as the material of permanence it is. Over fourteen years we have worked on countertops, bathrooms and residential floors as closely as we have worked on hotel lobbies and commercial atriums — restoring marble, granite and other natural stone without compromising craftsmanship or overrunning the budget.

What sets us apart is not only experience, but the proprietary systems developed along the way. Every recurring problem pushed us to engineer a more reliable way to solve it, so even the most challenging restorations are handled by a repeatable, dependable process rather than guesswork.
